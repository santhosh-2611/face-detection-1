Why is Template Matching not scale invariant? ＋
Template Matching compares the template at one fixed size. If the object becomes larger or smaller, the pixel pattern changes and the correlation score decreases.
Why are integral images useful in Viola-Jones? ＋
Integral images allow the sum of a rectangular region to be calculated using only four array lookups. This makes Haar feature calculation very fast.
What does DeepFace do? ＋
DeepFace detects the face, aligns it, extracts deep features, and compares the resulting representations to verify whether two faces belong to the same person.
What is triplet loss in FaceNet? ＋
Triplet loss brings the anchor and positive image closer in the embedding space while pushing the negative image farther away.
